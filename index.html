V6 Redline-Power Hybrid Turbo

A highly optimized, 1.6-litre internal combustion engine mated with state-of-the-art dual energy recovery units (MGU-K harvesting braking energy, and MGU-H converting turbo exhaust heat). This delivers seamless and continuous performance output, boasting unprecedented thermal efficiency.

RPM Max: 15,000 RPM
Turbochargers: Single Adaptive Stage
Fuel System: 350-bar Direct Injection
Battery Weight: 25 KG Lithium-Carbon

Active Aerodynamics & Air Management

The APX-06 utilizes Venturi tunnels underneath the chassis to create high levels of ground effect downforce without sacrificing high-end straight-line speed. The rear wing features a hydraulic Drag Reduction System (DRS), yielding a 35% drag reduction on straights.

DRS Activation: Under 0.2 Seconds
Peak Downforce: 2200 KG at 300 KM/H
Underfloor Layout: Twin Venturi Channels
Drag Coefficient (Cd): Dynamic-Adaptive

Carbon-Composite Monocoque Structure

Handcrafted with over 80 layered carbon-fiber weave patterns integrated with high-tensile Kevlar. The survival cell provides extreme torsional rigidity, safeguarding the pilot during impact scenarios while optimizing critical weight distribution across the base.

Material Grade: Grade A+ Carbon fiber
Impact Capacity: Up to 120 kN Force
Titanium Halo: Grade 5 Structural Alloy
Weight Balance: 45.4% Front / 54.6% Rear
